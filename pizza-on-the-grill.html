Pizza on the Grill

Home

Pizza on the grill is a fantastic way to make pizza at home! The heat of a hot grill is a perfect match for a professional pizza oven. This grilled pizza recipe makes the dough from scratch and flavors it with garlic and basil. Use your favorite variety of toppings and shredded cheese, but it's best not to overload the pizza.

No pizza oven? No problem! Make your homemade pizza on the grill for that classic smoky, wood fired oven flavor. And even if you have a pizza oven, you might prefer these grilled pizzas from time to time.

Ingredients

The following are the ingredients for making the pizza dough: 1 sup of warm water (110 degrees F), 1 (.25 ounce) package active dry yeast, 1 pinch white sugar, 3 1/3 cups of all-purpose flour, 1 tablespoon olive oil, teaspoons kosher salt, 2 cloves of minced garlic, and 1 tablespoon chooped fresh basil.

To make the garil oil that will be on the pizza crust, you need 1/2 cup of olive oil anf 1 teaspoon minced garlic.

For the pizza toppings you need 1/4 cup of divided tomato sauce, 1 cup of divided chopped tomatoes, 1/4 cup of sliced black olives, 1/4 cup roasted, drained and chopped red peppers, 2 cups of shredded mozzarella cheese, and 4 tablespoons of chopped basil.

Directions

  1. To stat making the pizza, gather all the ingredients
  2. Make dough: Pour warm water into a large bowl; dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water. Let stand until yeast softens and begins to form a creamy foam, about 5 to 10 minutes.
  3. Mix in flour, 1 tablespoon olive oil, and salt until dough pulls away from the side of the bowl.
  4. Turn dough onto a lightly floured surface. Knead until smooth, about 8 minutes
  5. PLce the dough in a well-oiled bowl and cover with a damp colth.
  6. Set aside to rise until doubled in sizefor about 1 hour. After doubled in size, take dough and punch don kneading in the garlic basil. Set aside to rise for 1 more hour, or until doubled again.
  7. Meanwhile, make the garlic oil: combine 1/2 cup of olive oil with mince garlic in a microwave-dafe cup or bowl. Heat for 30 seconds in the microwave.
  8. Preheat an outdor grill for high heatts; bruhsh the grate with the garlic oil.
  9. Make the pizzaz: punch down the dough and divide in half. Then form each half into an oblong chape 3/8 to 1/2 inch thick.
  10. Carefully place one piece of dough on the hot grill. The dough eill begin to puff almost immediately. When the bottom crust has light browned, turn dought over using two spatulas.
  11. Once the douhg is fliped over, quickly bruh the surface of the pizza with the garlic oil.
  12. Then, top the pizza with 1/2 of each of the following: tomato sauce, chopped tomatoes, olives, red peppers, cheese, and basil.
  13. Close the lid of the grill and cook until cheese melts. Once the cheese melts fullt, remove the pizza from the grill and set it aside to cool for a few minutes. Repeat with the second piece of dough.
  14. Finally, serve and enjoy!
