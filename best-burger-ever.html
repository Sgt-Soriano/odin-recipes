Best Hamburger Ever!

Home When you are at a BBQ and do not know what to make, you can use this recipe. This burger offers crispy, juicy, homemade delicacy that everyone at your backyard can enjoy.

Ingredients

Note: This recipe will not cover how to make good buns for your burgers, but they will only include the ingredients for the burger patty.

The following are the ingredients for the burger patty: 1 ½ pounds lean ground beef, ½ finely chopped onion, ½ cup shredded Colby Jack or Cheddar cheese, 1 large egg, 1 ounce of dry onion soup mix, 1 clove of minced garlic, 1 tablespoon of garlic powder, 1 teaspoon soy sauce, 1 teaspoon off Worcestershire sacue, 1 teaspoon of dried parsley, 1 teaspoon of basil, 1 teaspoon of dried oregano, 1/2 teaspoon oof crushed dried rosemary, and a pinch of salt and pepper.

Directions

  1. Gather all the ingredients. Preheat an outdoor grill for high heat and lightly oil the grate.
