Best Hamburger Ever!

Home

When you are at a BBQ and do not know what to make, you can use this recipe. This burger offers crispy, juicy, homemade delicacy that everyone at your backyard can enjoy.

Ingredients

Note: This recipe will not cover how to make good buns for your burgers, but they will only include the ingredients for the burger patty.

The following are the ingredients for the burger patty: 1 1/2 pounds lean ground beef, 1/2 finely chopped onion, 1/2 cup shredded Colby Jack or Cheddar cheese, 1 large egg, 1 ounce of dry onion soup mix, 1 clove of minced garlic, 1 tablespoon of garlic powder, 1 teaspoon soy sauce, 1 teaspoon off Worcestershire sacue, 1 teaspoon of dried parsley, 1 teaspoon of basil, 1 teaspoon of dried oregano, 1/2 teaspoon oof crushed dried rosemary, and a pinch of salt and pepper.

Directions

  1. Gather all the ingredients. Preheat an outdoor grill for high heat and lightly oil the grate.
  2. Meanwhile, combine the ground beef, onion, cheese, egg, onion soup mix, minced garlic, garlic powder, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, parsley, basil, oregano, rosemary, salt and pepper inlarge bowl
  3. Next, use your hands to make 4 patties and placee them onto a tray with parchment paper.
  4. Now, cook the patties on a pre-heated grill until the patties are no longer pink in the centre and the juice runs clear. Each side should take about 4 to 5 minute on each side.
  5. Next, use an instant read thermometer to check that the centre of the burger reads at least 165 degrees Farenheight.
  6. Finally, take your favorite brand of burger buns and server with your perfered topping. Enjoy!
