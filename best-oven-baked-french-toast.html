Best Oven Baked French Toast!

Home

Have you ever wanted to make French Toast but were intimidated by how difficult it might be? Well, fear no more! This recipe is very simple and easy to make . Some things that are to love about this recipe are that it uses regular sandwhich bread, you can change the amounts of the ingredients, and, for maximum flavor, add apples!

Ingredients

To make the batter for the bread you will need the ffollowing: 1/2 cup of melted butter, 3/4 cup of broown sugar, 1 tablespoon of ground cinnamon, 6 eggs, 1/2 cup of milk, and 1 pinch of salt.

Directions

  1. First off, before starting anything, grab your ingredients.
  2. Now, grab a 9x13-inch baking dish and pour the melted butter into the pan.
  3. Next, sprinkle the brown sugar cinnamon evenly over the melted butter. Then, arrange two layers of bread slices on top.
  4. Off to the side, whick the eggs, milk, and salt together in a bowl. Then, pour the contents onto the bread. Cover the pan and let it refrigerate overnight.
  5. After they have been refrigerate overnight, preheat an oven to 350 degrees Farenheight. Bake the toast in the oven until golden brown which takes about 30 minutes.
  6. After the 30 minutes are up, serve with your favorite toppings and enjoy!
